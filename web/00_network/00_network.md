
---
A **computer network** is a group of two or more computers or devices connected so they can **communicate and share information or resources**.


|Type|Full Form|Coverage|Typical Example|Main Use|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**PAN**|Personal Area Network|A few meters|Phone ↔ smartwatch|Personal devices|
|**LAN**|Local Area Network|Room/building/campus|Home/office network|Local resource sharing|
|**WLAN**|Wireless LAN|Same as LAN|Wi-Fi network|Wireless local connectivity|
|**CAN**|Campus Area Network|Several buildings|University campus|Connect multiple LANs|
|**MAN**|Metropolitan Area Network|City/metro area|ISP city network|Connect networks across a city|
|**WAN**|Wide Area Network|Country/continent/world|Corporate WAN|Connect geographically distant networks|
|**GAN**|Global Area Network|Worldwide|Global corporate network|Worldwide connectivity|
|**SAN**|Storage Area Network|Usually data center|Servers ↔ storage systems|High-speed storage access|
|**VPN**|Virtual Private Network|Logical/virtual|Employee → company network|Secure communication over another network|



|Model|Basic Idea|Example|Common Use|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Client-Server**|Clients request services from servers|Browser → Web Server|Web, databases, email|
|**Peer-to-Peer (P2P)**|Devices communicate directly as equals|Torrent clients|File sharing|
|**Master-Slave**|Master controls/manages workers|Controller → Devices|Industrial/legacy systems|
|**Distributed**|Work/data distributed across multiple nodes|Blockchain|Distributed systems|
|**Centralized**|One central system controls resources|Central authentication server|Enterprise systems|
|**Decentralized**|Control distributed among multiple nodes|Blockchain/P2P networks|Resilient systems|
|**Hybrid**|Combination of architectures|Modern cloud apps|Most real-world systems|
